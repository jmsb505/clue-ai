What gets sent when automatic Jev is enabled

After each search, Clue sends parsed profile fields (summary, target roles, skills, experience, education, and languages), the optional preferences entered for that search, and selected listing details (title, company, description, and employment type). It removes email addresses, phone numbers, and web links from profile text; it does not send the original CV file, work authorization, or source URL.

Work history and skills can still identify you. TypeSafe processes the request outside this device. Clue does not inspect your TypeSafe account terms, billing or refill settings, or other uses of the same key. The local ledger limits Clue's own reservations to the amount shown here; it cannot guarantee account-wide charges.

Jev scoring is currently limited to English profile details and listings that Clue can confidently identify as English. Other languages remain visible without a Jev score.

What a selected listing sends

OpenAI is called only after you click Prepare application on one listing and only when its Jev result is an eligible match. Clue sends that job description and read-only Jev result, the one CV you select, approved claims from permitted technical profiles and that CV, your saved writing preferences, and bounded public page text gathered by the Researcher. Up to three explicitly selected descriptive profiles or writing samples go only to the Rewriter; descriptive profiles guide owner-stated motivation and preferences, while samples are style cues only. Other CVs and unselected writing samples stay local. Public contact email addresses are kept local. The Researcher receives no CV, candidate claims, or profile. The Diagnoser, Recruiter, and Rewriter have no crawl, filesystem, Gmail, send, or submit tools.

Clue requests store:false. OpenAI documents that this prevents response storage for later retrieval, but default abuse-monitoring logs may still retain prompts and responses for up to 30 days unless your organization is approved for Modified Abuse Monitoring or Zero Data Retention. Check your account's current data controls and terms before enabling this consent.

SEPARATE OWNER-CONTROLLED ACTION

Gmail Drafts

After you approve an exact packet version, you may separately click Create Gmail draft for an outreach message with a publicly sourced email. Clue calls only Gmail's create-draft endpoint. It has no send, read, edit, or delete operation; you review and send from Gmail yourself.

Google's gmail.compose authorization scope technically also permits sending. Clue's adapter does not expose or call a send method. A draft-creation timeout is held as unresolved; Clue will not retry until you check Gmail Drafts and reconcile it.

The refresh token is encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows account and stored under ignored local .data/gmail/. Full local-data deletion removes it. Disconnecting Clue removes the local token; revoke Google's grant separately in your Google Account if desired.
