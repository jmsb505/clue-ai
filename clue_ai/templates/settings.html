What gets sent when automatic Jev is enabled

After each search, Clue sends parsed profile fields (summary, target roles, skills, experience, education, and languages), the optional preferences entered for that search, and selected listing details (title, company, description, and employment type). It removes email addresses, phone numbers, and web links from profile text; it does not send the original CV file, work authorization, or source URL.

Work history and skills can still identify you. TypeSafe processes the request outside this device. Clue does not inspect your TypeSafe account terms, billing or refill settings, or other uses of the same key. The local ledger limits Clue's own reservations to the amount shown here; it cannot guarantee account-wide charges.

Jev scoring is currently limited to English profile details and listings that Clue can confidently identify as English. Other languages remain visible without a Jev score.
