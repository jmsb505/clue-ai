02 · ADD A REVIEWED LEAD

Save the employer's job page

Saved on this device

Open the X post and follow the lead in your browser. When you find the original employer or ATS vacancy, copy its direct page URL and the job details here. Clue will not test either link.

Before saving a link
  • Check the address bar and confirm the final page belongs to the employer or a recognizable recruiting system.
  • Confirm the role is a real vacancy and read where the employer can hire, especially whether Italy is allowed.
  • Do not download files, pay a fee, or enter account credentials because an X post asks you to.

Clue rejects non-HTTPS, local/IP, credential-bearing, and common shortened job URLs. A valid-looking URL can still be misleading.

Clue saves the two links and job details locally. It makes no Jev request during import.
