Clue is a local personal job-search tool. It does not host your profile, sell your information, or apply to jobs for you.

⌂●
01

Your CV and profile

PDF and DOCX files are parsed on this computer. Your editable profile, original CV, local job index, search history, and saved roles are stored under the project's ignored .data/ directory. Scanned PDFs are not sent to an OCR provider.

02

Public job sources

Search refreshes only approved feeds and pages on their allowed schedule. It keeps source names, publisher credit, original links, posted dates, and check times. Listings remain local and are not submitted to another job board.

03

TypeSafe Jev

Jev does not run during ordinary searches. If you configure a key, review the disclosure, enable Jev, and click “Score with Jev”, Clue sends reviewed job-related profile fields, search preferences, and selected listing details. The raw CV, direct contacts, work authorization, and source URL are excluded. Work history can still identify you.

04

Your controls

Remove the original CV from My Profile, turn Jev off in Settings, pause a source, or delete the local profile and job-search data. A deletion cannot remove data already processed by TypeSafe. Clue does not inspect your TypeSafe account settings or verify account-wide charges; its local reserve applies only to requests made by Clue.
