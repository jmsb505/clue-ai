## Product interpretation

A useful cover letter introduces the application, develops relevant candidate contributions with context or approach and outcomes where known, explains the particular application rationale, and closes professionally. These functions can share paragraphs. There is no absolute word, paragraph, or section count. Around one page is a practical industry norm. A longer letter is not automatically better, but a few compressed factual notes are insufficient when substantial useful evidence is available.

Personal motivation must come from permitted owner evidence; it cannot be inferred from an employer website. Ordinary requests to discuss an application and thanks are communicative acts. All surrounding career facts and commitments still require evidence. Candidate project brands and repository names are omitted from generated prose in favor of accurate descriptive labels; original source metadata remains private for traceability.

## Failure diagnosed

The previous live run produced a 98-word body that passed automated reviews after a complementary example was removed. Its relevance was real, but its argument was underdeveloped. Duplicated prompt rules encouraged project-fact/job-duty pairs, allowed a conclusion to disappear, and overemphasized brevity. Factual support alone cannot establish a complete letter.

## Correction and acceptance

Prompt .72 removes the duplicated cover-letter guidance and replaces it with the researched content functions. The reviewer must judge the visible explanation and professional conclusion, cite concrete wording in its reasons, and evaluate omitted complementary evidence. Jev retains factual authority and saved matching decisions remain immutable. Jev guidance distinguishes courteous communicative acts from unsupported facts and permits accurate descriptive labels.

Acceptance requires focused and full regression checks, a manually triggered live packet using actual bound profiles, Jev-supported final content, independent reading of the complete letter, and exact-artifact rendering. Results are recorded below; unknown is not a pass. No application or outreach is sent.
