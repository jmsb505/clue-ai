### Letter failure diagnosis and workflow changes

- **Accenture:** both paragraphs failed Jev support. One joined a project description to a role requirement with an unsupported first-person contribution and an unsupported project label; the other used a project as evidence for a role connection that the cited source did not state.
- **Bending Spoons:** one paragraph passed; the other presented an inferred project-to-role relationship as a candidate fact, so Jev left it unresolved. The packet correctly failed the 2/2 requirement.
- **IFOM:** prompt `.16` passed 2/2 support, but both paragraphs reused a generic relevance-bridge pattern. The rendered letter also lacked a greeting and closing.
- **Root cause:** the prompt asked for a connection between candidate and role, which incentivized synthesis beyond the cited facts. The allowed “return only supported paragraphs” fallback conflicted with the fixed two-paragraph gate. The renderer left document scaffolding to the generated body.

M6 changed the process rather than weakening the gate: the initial Rewriter now states candidate facts and job responsibilities separately, preserves source project names and contribution verbs, and avoids unsupported fit/transfer claims. After initial Jev review, one GPT-6 Luna repair can touch only the rejected or exact-duplicate paragraph indices. Any initially supported paragraph is locked. Clue binds replacements to the approved evidence and sends the complete final letter back to Jev. The two-paragraph/2-of-2 support gate and final-CV approval gate still control whether documents are created. Clue now supplies the generic greeting/closing locally and uses a name only from a clear CV header.

### Evaluation and opinion

- **Matching and separation:** Pass for this small sample. Jev kept IFOM and Accenture at `review`, Bending Spoons at `match`, and eligible location; generation never changed those results. This is not personal-fit calibration.
- **Evidence safety:** Pass for the `.38` IFOM packet. Jev supported both final letter paragraphs and separately approved the CV. An invalid evidence ID is now dropped at block level before Jev; the rest still faces Jev, and an incomplete letter still fails closed.
- **Writing:** Both failed postings now have review packets after a targeted repair and full Jev recheck. The letters are grounded and concise, but visually sparse and not persuasive enough to call final: role facts and candidate facts are often juxtaposed rather than connected. A separate quality review must remain distinct from the factual support gate. The owner still needs to assess the actual packet against the direct, modest, specific voice guidelines.
- **CV value:** Not demonstrated. Recruiter found partial or absent coverage across the jobs, but no run retained a CV edit. Jev approval establishes no material issue under its review rubric; it does not establish that the CV is better tailored or more competitive. This is the largest remaining quality gap.
- **Research and outreach:** Best-effort and inconsistent. The two M6 retries found zero contacts; Bending also returned zero accepted research findings. An official IFOM recruitment inbox and one Jev-supported unsent draft were found on an earlier retry. Contact discovery should be treated as best-effort, not a guaranteed output.
- **Documents:** The M6 Accenture and Bending Spoons cover letters reopened in Microsoft Word, exported through Word, and rendered with Poppler. Both are one page with the new greeting and closing and no clipping/overlap, but both are substantially underfilled. The earlier IFOM `.16` letter lacks that later scaffold; its resume's second page was sparse. Failed requests did not expose partial documents.
- **Filtering:** Added a regression for the Accenture-style case where an `Analyst` title describes hands-on AI implementation, while retaining a negative generic-analyst case. Both pass offline; no ATS behavior is claimed.

The pipeline now repairs one unsupported letter paragraph when evidence supports a replacement, reruns the full final letter through Jev, and tunes a measured Researcher output limit. Live retests confirmed this path on Accenture; Bending Spoons completed after the Researcher adjustment. This evaluation still does **not** establish a reliably tailored CV, persuasive cover letters, repeatable outreach research, personal-voice acceptance, ATS performance, or hiring benefit. All packets remain local and in owner review.
