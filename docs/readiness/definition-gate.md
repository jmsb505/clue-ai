## Checks before using a real CV or relying on rankings

These items separate owner-waived checks from evidence that still matters to ranking quality or individual source access.

1. The owner waived TypeSafe account/terms verification on 2026-09-30. Account-specific credit conversion, refills, charges, retention, telemetry, and deletion remain unverified. The local app limits only its own requests and retains an explicit disclosure/opt-in and per-search scoring action.
2. The initial free source set is selected and its feeds were smoke-checked; M2 added a transient Lever API/page parser comparison. Continue source-by-source terms validation before enabling any employer/ATS board. The [source review](../research/source-discovery-and-crawl-review.md) records coverage, limits, and alternatives with conflicting or incomplete terms.
3. The owner waived device-encryption confirmation for local `.data/` and backups. Local deletion and manual backup/restore are documented and tested with synthetic data; no hosted account, authentication service, or shared deployment is planned. Windows file permissions were not independently inspected.
4. Revisit privacy or employment/AI review only if the owner later offers the app to other users.
5. Review the synthetic benchmark examples and provide personal relevance judgments, or explicitly defer personal calibration before relying on rankings.

## Gate decision

The **definition gate passes for a single-user local feasibility build**. The Milan/Italy remote search focus, user inputs, local data boundary, five-source strategy, crawler behavior, budget, and Jev's role are defined in the product definition, source review, ADRs, and [PLAN-001](../plans/PLAN-001-free-local-source-discovery.md). The owner waived provider-side terms/account verification and local-device encryption confirmation; neither is claimed as verified. All non-TypeSafe costs remain $0, and the app still requires explicit Jev opt-in and a separate score action.

This gate does not authorize a public deployment. Source-specific conditions still apply before enabling optional employer/ATS sources. Do not bypass site controls or exceed the app's Jev reserve; actual account-wide charges are not verified.
